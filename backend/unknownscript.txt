Scene-by-scene script
Scene 1 — 0–3 seconds: Product Introduction

A young woman stands in a bright, elegant bathroom with soft natural sunlight coming through the window. She gently picks up a moisturizing cream jar from the countertop and looks at it with a subtle smile. The camera slowly pushes in toward the product.

Scene 2 — 3–7 seconds: Product Application

Cut to a medium close-up. The woman opens the jar, takes a small amount of cream with her fingertip, and gently applies it to her cheek. Her movements are natural and graceful. Soft lighting highlights her skin, while the background remains clean and slightly blurred.

Scene 3 — 7–10 seconds: Final Product Shot

The woman smiles confidently at the camera while holding the cream jar beside her face. The camera shifts focus from her face to the product. End with a clear, centered product shot against the elegant bathroom background.
